\section{Feature Selection for Recognition of PQDs}
The eight features in~\autoref{tab:extracted_features} were extracted from the PQD signals using S-transform and statistical analysis. A machine learning model can use these features to classify the PQDs into their respective classes. However, not all features may be equally important for the classification of every PQD class. Thus, as a first step, feature selection is performed to identify the most relevant features for each PQD class.
